PENCLASH

Flick it. Spin it. Knock their pen off the paper.

How to play
  1. On your turn, press anywhere on the arena, drag back like a slingshot and let go. Your pen shoots the opposite way.
  2. The spot on your pen nearest to where you pressed is where you strike it: press near an end to add spin.
  3. Knock other pens past the dashed edge. Fall off and you are out for the round. Last pen standing wins the round.
  4. Pick up boosters on the arena: Freeze makes you hard to move until your next turn, Speed powers up your next shot, Fire makes your next shot hit extra hard. Hit a pen 3 times in the same spot and it snaps: PEN BREAK floods the arena with slippery ink.
  5. Every round you pick a new pen. First to 3 rounds wins. 2 to 4 players. Heavy pens hit harder, light pens fly further. Pick an arena: ice is slippery, erasers bounce you back.
